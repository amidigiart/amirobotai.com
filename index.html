Frequently asked questions

How do I choose between a cobot and a traditional industrial robot?
Cobots (collaborative robots) are best for tasks near humans, payloads under 35kg, and flexible production. Traditional industrial robots handle heavy payloads (up to 2300kg), high speed, and high-volume repetitive tasks. Key factors: payload, speed, safety requirements (ISO 10218 vs ISO/TS 15066), and production volume.
What RPA tools are best for small businesses?
For small businesses: UiPath Community (free for small teams), Power Automate (if using Microsoft 365), n8n (open-source, self-hosted). Start with high-volume, rule-based processes like invoice processing or data entry. ROI typically visible within 3-6 months.
How does predictive maintenance with IoT work?
Sensors (vibration, temperature, current) stream data via MQTT or OPC-UA to an edge gateway. ML models detect anomaly patterns before failure. Typical stack: sensor → edge gateway → time-series DB → anomaly detection → alert system. Reduces unplanned downtime by 30-50%.
What safety standards apply to cobots?
ISO/TS 15066 governs collaborative robot safety. It defines four collaboration types: safety-rated monitored stop, hand guiding, speed and separation monitoring, and power and force limiting. Risk assessment per ISO 12100 is always required. CE marking mandatory in EU.
